bonsai

A mobile Markdown viewer & editor for your own Google Drive.

bonsai lets you read and edit the Markdown (.md) files you keep in your own Google Drive, naturally, from your phone. Your Drive folder structure stays exactly as you organized it — bonsai simply gives you a comfortable mobile reading and writing experience on top of it.

What bonsai does

  • Browse your Drive folders — navigate your existing folder hierarchy with a list or tree view, filtered to Markdown files.
  • Read Markdown beautifully — open any .md file in a clean, distraction-free preview.
  • Edit and auto-save — switch to the editor, make changes, and bonsai automatically saves them back to the same file in your Drive.
  • Create new notes — add new Markdown files to any folder, right where you want them.

How it works with your data

bonsai is a client-only app with no backend server. All Google Drive access happens directly between your device and Google. Your files stay in your own Drive; bonsai keeps no copy of them anywhere. Sign-in uses Google OAuth, and you can revoke bonsai's access at any time from your Google Account settings.

For full details, see the bonsai Privacy Policy.

Availability

bonsai is an Android app distributed via Google Play (currently in testing ahead of public release). An iOS version is planned.
