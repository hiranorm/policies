komadori

A little bird that listens to lectures for you — and brings them back as Markdown notes.

komadori records lectures, seminars, and talks, transcribes them on your device, and formats the transcript into readable Markdown notes in real time. It is built for people who read faster than they listen: instead of a raw recording, you take home structured knowledge you can actually re-read.

What komadori does

  • Record and transcribe on-device — speech recognition runs locally on your phone. No audio leaves your device for transcription, and it works offline.
  • Format as you go — the transcript is shaped into Markdown in small chunks while you record, so a readable note grows in real time.
  • Keep the audio — recordings are saved locally (.m4a) so you can replay any part of a session.
  • Upload to your Google Drive (optional) — when a note is done, you can upload it to a folder you choose in your own Google Drive, so it joins the knowledge folders you already maintain.

How it works with your data

komadori is a client-only app with no backend server. Recording, transcription, and Markdown formatting all happen on your device. The only network activity is the optional Google Drive upload, which goes directly between your device and Google — the developer never receives or stores your data. Sign-in uses Google OAuth, and you can revoke komadori's access at any time from your Google Account settings.

For full details, see the komadori Privacy Policy.

Availability

komadori is an Android app distributed via Google Play (currently in testing ahead of public release). An iOS version is planned.
